# Throughout the course of Persona 5, your mentor and ward Sashiro Sakura teaches 
# you to make the signature dish of his restaurant, Leblanc's Cafe.
# Leblanc's Curry is a rich dish, made from a secret recipe (and by 
# following your heart), and always served with freshly brewed coffee. 
# Eating it outside of battle restores 30 SP to all party members. 

# Atlus eventually published a recipe card for the curry online, which Binging 
# with Babish helpfully adapted.

# The original recipe is made with beef, so we'll have to make some adaptations to 
# capture the magic in a vegan kind of way.


#Original recipe with beef (from this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJs3rq2keQ&pp=0gcJCfwAo7VqN5tD)


Ingredients:

- 1 lb of beef, cut into 1-2 inch cubes
- 2 onions, finely chopped 
- 1 peeled carrot, cut into jewel shapes ('roll cut')
- 1/2 peeled and grated apple
- 1 inch of ginger, peeled and cut, grated
- 1 clove of garlic, grated
-1 cup of red wine

- 1 tbsp of vegetable oil 

Prep Meat: 
1. Heat vegetable oil, sear beef on all sides until brown
3. Season beef with salt and pepper, 1 cup red wine to deglaze
4. once deglazed, add 3 cups of water, simmer until boiling
3. Add carrots

Prep Veggies:
2. While beef is searing, Caramelize onions in another pan
3. Once onions are brown on all sides, add apple, ginger, 
and garlic until brown (add carrot to beef)
- then work on roux


Prep Roux:
- in a small pan, add flour and toast 36 grams of all puprose
- then add 2 tsps of cumin, coriander, tumeric, 1 tsp of cardamom, cinnamon, chili powder1/2 tsp nutmet, ground clove
- 1/4 tsp black pepper. stir and toast until flour is perfumed, 
then turn off Heat let to cool

Put it all together:
- once beef is at a nice simmer, add veggies + a bay leaf. deglaze the veggie pan with a little bit of the simmering liquid, and add that to the curry
- once verything is happy, stir in the flour and stir in all the lumps
- simmer on 1 hour on low heat, stir every once in a while
- remove bay leaf, through in 1 tbsp of bouillon, 1 tbsp worstershire sauce, 
, 2 tbsps honey 2 g of instant coffee, 8g of dark chocoloate, stir in
- take off heat, stir in 2 tbsps of butter and 110 g of plain yogurt

Serve:
- make coffee and serve with curry over rice

Steps in Order:
